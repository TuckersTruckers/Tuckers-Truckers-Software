OUR STORY

Built on Understanding How Freight Really Moves

Tucker’s Truckers grew from a lifelong dedication to logistics and a commitment to improving the experience of independent carriers.

The journey began in high school working for a moving company, first as a helper and later as a driver and crew chief. That hands-on experience led to independent contracting, driving trucks and installing furniture and appliances for companies including Lowe’s and Restoration Hardware.

Alongside that work came a warehouse role with Vail Resorts, which eventually led to a full-time position as a logistics coordinator. That experience built a foundation in planning, coordination, and the details that keep complex operations moving.

From there, the path moved into freight brokerage. Working directly with brokers, shippers, and carriers developed a practical understanding of market behavior, rate negotiation, and the importance of clear communication. Shipment details and accessorial expectations matter—and they should be discussed before a truck is committed.

Brokerage also revealed how easily drivers can miss fair compensation for detention, specialized equipment, and other accessorials when details are overlooked or loads are booked too quickly.

That experience led back to working alongside carriers as a dispatcher. Today, Tucker’s Truckers combines those perspectives to support competitive freight decisions, proper compensation, better organization, and stronger day-to-day operations.
